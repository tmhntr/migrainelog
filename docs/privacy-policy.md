# Privacy Policy — MigraineLog

_Last updated: 12 August 2026_

MigraineLog ("the app") is a personal migraine tracking app for iOS. This policy
explains how the app handles your information.

## Summary

**MigraineLog does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data.** Everything
you enter stays on your device.

## What data the app stores

The app stores the information you choose to enter — migraine triggers, episodes,
treatments, and related notes — in a database on your device. This includes health-
related information such as symptoms and severity.

## Where your data is stored

All data is stored **locally on your device** using the device's built-in database
(SQLite). The app:

- Makes **no network requests** and has **no backend server**.
- Requires **no account, login, or registration**.
- Contains **no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs**.
- Does **not** share your data with the developer or any third party.

## Data you control

Because your data lives only on your device:

- You can permanently delete all app data at any time via **Settings → Clear All Data**.
- Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.
- Your data is included in your device's own backups (e.g. iCloud or iTunes/Finder
  backups) if you have those enabled. Those backups are governed by Apple's privacy
  policy, not this one. The developer has no access to them.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
