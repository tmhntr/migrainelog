Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your device. MigraineLog has no account and no server — nothing you enter ever leaves your phone. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I back up my data?

Your MigraineLog data is included in your normal device backup (iCloud or iTunes/Finder). There is no separate export in this version.

How do I delete all my data?

Open the app, go to Settings → Clear All Data. This is permanent. Deleting the app also removes all its data.

Does MigraineLog predict or diagnose migraines?

No. MigraineLog is a personal tracking tool. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a qualified healthcare provider for medical advice.
